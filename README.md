Study Tracker
A simple offline study-hours tracker that installs like an app on your phone or laptop.
Set a daily goal, time your study sessions, and see your progress over time.
Features
Daily goal with a progress ring
Start/stop timer, Pomodoro mode and a distraction-free focus screen
Manual time entry, including past days
Missed time carries over to the next day (with a cap, so it never snowballs)
Rest days that never break your streak
Courses with exam countdowns and optional daily goals
Progress screen: 7-day chart, streaks, weekly and all-time totals, hours per course
Edit or remove sessions
Backup export and import, with a weekly reminder
Dark mode that follows your device
Works fully offline once installed
